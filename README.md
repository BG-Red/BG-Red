### ⚡ About Me

I am **BG-Red** (Zach Pierce), a **Full-Stack Systems Architect & Product Engineer** specializing in turning ambitious technical visions into production-grade software. My engineering spans autonomous AI agent systems, high-concurrency SaaS platforms, and performance-critical cross-platform mobile apps.

From architecting multi-agent incident-triage runbooks to engineering 60 FPS vector diagramming engines and enterprise e-commerce pipelines, I focus on deterministic systems design, rock-solid DX, and zero bloat.

- 🔭 **Currently Engineering:** Autonomous IT operations at [flowrmm.com](https://flowrmm.com) & sports tech SaaS at [youthsportsos.com](https://youthsportsos.com).
- 🎯 **Core Competencies:** Full-Stack Architecture, Autonomous AI Agents (MCP), Native Cross-Platform Apps (React Native / Flutter), Cloud & DevOps.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Model Context Protocol (MCP), Skia 2D graphics, Supabase/Firebase architectures, low-latency client-server sync.
- ⚡ **Philosophy:** *"Ship fast, architect for resilience, and never compromise on user experience."*
